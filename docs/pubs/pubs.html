Braun, K.N., Andresen, C.G. 2024. Heterogeneity in ice-wedge permafrost revealed across spatial scales. Remote Sensing of Envrionment. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rse.2024.114299

  • My Master’s thesis! My goal was to combine geospatial analysis, drone data, and fieldwork to better understand the spatial patterns in permafrost thaw.
  • Mapped hot and cold spots of ice-wedge thaw in high-resolution Maxar imagery
  • Introduced hydrogeomorphology technique for classifying thaw stages
  • Found most severe thaw clusters on high elevation portions of coastal plain

Braun, K.N., Mattheus, C.R., Theuerkauf, E.J. 2022. The geomorphic role of large wood in the coastal zone of the North American Great Lakes. Geomorphology. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.geomorph.2022.108292

  • My last project at the Illinois State Geological Survey :’) When COVID shut down fieldwork, I spent a lot of time staring at our record of drone surveys, thinking about driftwood, and devised this study to figure out if all the trees that had been felled during high water levels were impacting beach processes
  • Tracked a decade of coastal large wood dynamics using aerial and drone imagery
  • Identified common transportation pathways through coastal zone
  • Observed only transient, seasonal impacts on shoreline geomorphology during period of rising water level (2009-2020)
